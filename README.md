# 🕌 DeenPoint

### Your Islamic Knowledge Hub

**DeenPoint** is a modern Islamic knowledge platform designed to make the **Holy Quran, authentic Hadith, and Islamic educational content** accessible in one simple and user-friendly place.

🔗 **Live Website:** [DeenPoint](https://umarbinriaz.github.io/Deen-Point/index.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## ✨ Features

### 📖 The Noble Quran

* Browse all **114 Surahs**
* Explore **6,236 Ayat**
* Search Surahs by name or Juz
* Listen to Quran recitations with an integrated audio player
* Easy-to-use Quran reading experience

### 📜 Hadith Collection

* Collection of **100 authentic Hadith**
* Original Arabic text
* Urdu translations
* Hadith organized by topic
* Daily Hadith feature for regular learning

### 📚 Islamic Knowledge

Explore **25 educational articles** covering topics such as:

* Tawheed
* Islamic History
* Fiqh & Jurisprudence
* Duas & Azkar
* Ramadan
* Quran Study
* Patience & Gratitude
* Family & Society
* Spiritual Growth

### 🎧 Audio Learning

Listen to Quran recitations while reading, making it easier to learn, reflect, and engage with the Quran.

## 🎯 Purpose

DeenPoint aims to provide a simple digital space where Muslims can:

* 📖 Read and explore the Quran
* 🤲 Learn authentic Hadith
* 🧠 Increase Islamic knowledge
* 🌙 Learn about worship and spirituality
* ❤️ Reflect on Islamic teachings
* 📚 Discover useful educational resources

The goal is to make Islamic learning **simple, accessible, and organized for everyday use**.
